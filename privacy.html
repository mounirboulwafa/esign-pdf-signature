      Privacy Policy

      Last updated: January 2026

      Your privacy is the foundation of eSign. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your documents and data.

      Everything happens on your device

      eSign processes every document entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Your PDFs, signatures, and edits never leave your device unless you choose to export or share them yourself.

      We don't collect your data

      eSign does not collect, store, or transmit your documents, signatures, or personal information to any server. There are no user accounts and no sign-in required.

      No third-party analytics or tracking

      eSign does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Your usage of the app is never monitored or sold.

      Contact us

      If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out using the contact form below.
