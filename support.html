      Common questions

      Does eSign work without an internet connection?

      Yes. eSign works completely offline — signing, editing, and saving all happen on your device.

      Is eSign available on iPad?

      Yes. eSign is built for both iPhone and iPad.

      Where are my signatures stored?

      Your signatures and documents are stored locally on your device only.

      How do I report a bug or request a feature?
